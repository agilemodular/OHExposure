# Professional review handover

**For:** the competent occupational hygienist, once appointed (gates **G2**, **G3** and **G4**).
**Engine:** 1.3.1. **Prepared:** 19 October 2026.
**Status:** no method is approved. Until you approve a method version, organisations at pilot level cannot submit, approve or issue conclusions that use it; the server and the database both refuse. Everything you need is listed here, so no code inspection is required.

> ## Read first: what the software does not judge
>
> **Representativeness and statistics are not assessed by the software.**
> - The engine calculates each worker-shift separately. It does not test whether the samples represent the group, the task variability or day-to-day variation.
> - It performs **no statistical compliance test** (for example BS EN 689 / EN 689 approaches, upper tolerance limits or exceedance fractions).
> - A result *below the benchmark* for the samples taken is not evidence that the group is adequately controlled.
>
> The practitioner's interpretation and your approval must address these questions. Conventions C18 and C19 ask you to accept this limitation for the pilot or require changes first.

## 2. Reference data

Pilot substances are chosen by the pilot organisation (decision pack P4). For each one, the template in `docs/templates/reference-data.template.json` is completed from the **current authoritative source**, including the edition and URL. Reproduction rights are checked and recorded in `licence_note`. **You verify every row** (`reviewer_name`, `verified_on`); you must not be the person who prepares or imports the file. The importer refuses the template as it stands, rejects synthetic or incomplete professional rows, and never edits an existing version.

## 3. How your decision becomes effective

1. You sign this handover and the validation pack (section 9 there), stating your name, competence, scope, any conditions and any review or expiry date.
2. A platform operator, who is not you, records the decision for each method version:

   ```
   platform-controls.ts method-review --method twa_8h --version 1.2.1 --status approved|approved_with_conditions|rejected \
     --reviewer "<you>" --competence "<competence>" --reviewed-on <date> --evidence "<document ref>" \
     --evidence-file <signed PDF> --scope "<permitted scope>" [--expires-on <date>] [--conditions "<text>"] --recorded-by "<operator>"
   ```

   The SHA-256 of the signed file is stored with the decision. The database refuses an approval recorded without a scope and a document hash.
3. **Withdrawal or supersession:** a later decision for the same version (`--status withdrawn`, or a new approval) takes effect for **new** use immediately. Approved assessments and issued reports keep the status recorded when they were approved. Those assessments show a notice saying the method's status has changed, and `platform-controls.ts method-impact` lists them for review. Conclusions are never rewritten.
4. **A new engine version** (any change to a formula, flag or roll-up) has a new method version and needs a new decision. Your approval does not carry over.
