## 6. Online-only configuration and test results

**Desktop Chromium check: 17/17.** It covers the eight required behaviours:
- offline capture cannot be enabled by users;
- no caches or device storage;
- earlier local data handled explicitly;
- a clear not-saved state, including a form that survives a dropped connection;
- nothing shown as saved without the server's acknowledgement;
- no duplicates after a lost response or a double submission;
- no automatic replay on reconnection;
- guidance for interrupted fieldwork.

**Re-check by a separate AI review agent (R3; not a human or external review):** all eight pass after the fixes. **This is not device evidence.** Online-only operation does not remove device, browser or privacy risks (`online-only-pilot-configuration.md` §4).
