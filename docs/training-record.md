# Pilot training record

**Gate:** G10. Fill in one record per person before they use the pilot environment. **Blank template: no one has been trained yet.** Keep completed records with the pilot file. Training is given in a training organisation or the demonstration organisation, never with pilot records.

## 1. Training sessions

| Session | Content | Roles | Duration (suggested) |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Platform basics | *For everyone* section of `pilot-user-guides.md`: purpose and limits, the three states, method status and provenance, personal data, how to report a problem and the stop conditions | All | 45 min |
| T2 Field capture | Field technician guide; device PIN, lock, sync states, conflicts, clock warnings, online-only mode, recovery file, shared devices; hands-on on the actual pilot device | Field technician, practitioner | 60 min |
| T3 Register, planning and laboratory | Practitioner guide §1–4; CSV import with a test file; QC dispositions | Practitioner | 90 min |
| T4 Assessment and reporting | Practitioner guide §5–9; reviewer guide; one worked assessment end to end, including a hand calculation | Practitioner, technical reviewer | 90 min |
| T5 Controls and verification | Action owner guide; reviewer guide §5; why "installed" is not "effective" | Action owner, practitioner, reviewer | 30 min |
| T6 Administration | Administrator guide; user lifecycle, two-step reset, retention, disposal, System health | Organisation administrator | 45 min |
| T7 Operator controls | Platform operator section; stop-condition drill (suspend and resume in a test organisation) | Platform operator | 45 min |
| T8 Data quality, suspension and escalation | *Data-quality flags* and *When something is suspended or on hold* in `pilot-user-guides.md`; reading a report; remediation intake and admission; held field records | All (field technicians: flags, suspension and escalation only) | 45 min |

## 2. Competence check (practical, observed by the trainer)

| # | Task | Roles | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Explain the three states and why none of them is a verdict | All | Correct, in own words |
| C2 | Name the stop conditions and say who to call | All | All seven named, or the card located; contact known |
| C3 | Capture an interrupted personal sample offline, then sync it | Field technician | Synchronised; interruption correct |
| C4 | Lock the device, unlock it, export a recovery file | Field technician | Done without help |
| C5 | Import a laboratory CSV with a non-detect | Practitioner | Validated and committed; the non-detect shown as an interval |
| C6 | Hand-calculate one 8-hour TWA and compare it with the platform | Practitioner, reviewer | Agrees within 0.1 % |
| C7 | Approve an assessment with scope and limitations; explain why a pilot approval may be refused | Reviewer | Correct |
| C8 | Record a not-effective review and explain what happens to the action | Reviewer | Correct |
| C9 | Suspend and later reactivate a user; reset two-step after an identity check | Administrator | Correct |
| C10 | Suspend and resume a workflow in a test organisation, with references | Operator | Audit events present |
| C11 | Explain three data-quality flags and an *indeterminate* comparison on a sample report | Practitioner, reviewer, client | Correct |
| C12 | During a test suspension: upload through remediation intake, then (as a different reviewer, after resumption) admit it; clear a held field record | Practitioner, reviewer | Done; audit shows intake, admission and clearance |
